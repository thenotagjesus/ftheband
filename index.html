FT Forget The Band

A Windows jam app with a Rocksmith-style follower band and a Reaper-style DAW spine. You play. The band stays with you. Then you treat the take like a real session.

  • 01 Follower band A backing band that locks to you — not a backing track you chase.
  • 02 DAW spine Tracks, timeline, record / punch / loop. The session is the product.
  • 03 Per-track VST3 Your plugins, on your tracks. Mixer and bounce when you’re done being loud.
  • 04 Printed chord names Original look. Chords on the page, not hidden in a mystery HUD.
